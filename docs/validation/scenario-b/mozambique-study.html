Recorded limitations

  • All plotted regional comparisons use the same pixels valid on every selected date or pair. Per-date coverage is shown separately; missing coverage is never reported as zero change.
  • Backscatter changes can reflect moisture, vegetation, roughness or geometry; they do not identify a cause by themselves.
  • Water candidates use a user-controlled threshold. They require local independent validation; shadow and smooth dry ground can be mistaken for water.
  • Area uses the common valid analysis grid; local UTM areas are approximate. Coarse analysis cells are averages, not new high-resolution observations.
  • Dated water playback uses the same common valid footprint and threshold on every date; it shows candidates, not verified floods or daily water evolution.
  • Baseline is the median backscatter of observations before the comparison date. Automatic comparison selection finds the largest consecutive candidate expansion; it does not verify a flood. Last-wet and first-dry observations bound the first observed return to dry; intervening wet/dry cycles are unknown, and no later dry observation means unresolved, not still flooded.
  • Input is a local native-grid area extract, not a complete original NASA product; only the saved rectangle can be analyzed.
  • GCOV source-sample mask is applied before averaging. It is not a terrain-shadow or water classifier.
